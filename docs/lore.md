# Lore summary

Human civilization is extinct. Sentient machines discover a frozen human prison and well-preserved biological remains. They convert the site into a laboratory to study
whether **true consciousness** can be restored, not merely bodies that mimic humans.

## The experiment

Reconstructed humans perform conditioned roles (guards, patrols, aggression) but lack full self-awareness. The machines hypothesize that consciousness requires agency,
uncertainty, threat and persistent choice. The prison becomes a controlled cognitive trial: a specimen wakes in a cell, is not told what they are and is given an apparent escape route that is itself part of the test.

## Protagonist

No known name at start. Begins with **fists** . HUD face portrait is narratively important because other humanoids share similar features, revealed gradually.

## Machines

Not generic villains. Motivated by scientific curiosity, archaeology, loneliness, and philosophical questions about biological consciousness. Internal disagreement may
surface in late game.

## Ending direction

Protagonist achieves genuine autonomy, understands the experiment, and destroys thecomplex. Central question: if consciousness was cultivated by captors, are they
creators, jailers or both?
